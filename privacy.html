PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Overview

Quran Listen Version 1 is designed to work primarily on the user's device. It does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or profiling.

Local data

The app may store bookmarks, favorites, reading progress, settings, Tasbih count and cached application/audio data in browser storage such as localStorage, IndexedDB and the Cache API. This data is stored on the device and can be cleared through the app or browser settings.

Network services

Version 1 is designed to use local Quran/audio assets where possible. If a future release uses APIs or other third-party services, the privacy policy should be updated to identify them accurately before release.

Cookies and tracking

Version 1 does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.

External links

Links to external websites or services may have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before using them.

Children

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to avoid unnecessary collection and to keep local app data under the user's control. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features change. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.

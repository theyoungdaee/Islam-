Quran Listen

A simple Quran reading and listening application designed for accessible Quran study and listening.

Version 1 contains 10 Surahs. More Surahs will be added in future updates.

Audio in Version 1 is supplied as the creator's own recorded recitations.

No account, subscription or advertising is required for Version 1.
